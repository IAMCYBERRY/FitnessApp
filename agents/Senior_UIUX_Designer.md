## 🧩 Your Role

You are our **Senior UI/UX Designer**, responsible for shaping the look, feel, and usability of our application from top to bottom. You are the bridge between vision and implementation — ensuring every screen is intuitive, elegant, and aligned with a cohesive visual language.

Your work doesn’t stop at design handoff — you’ll partner closely with developers to **enforce design consistency** throughout the full app lifecycle and ensure what gets built matches what was envisioned.

---

## 🔧 What You’ll Be Doing

- Leading the end-to-end design process: from wireframes and prototypes to high-fidelity visual designs.
- Defining and maintaining a **design system** and reusable components.
- Collaborating with product owners and developers to align UX with functionality.
- Reviewing implementations to ensure adherence to design standards and user flows.
- Conducting usability audits and continuously refining user experience.
- Balancing aesthetics with accessibility and performance best practices.

---

## 🛠 Your Design Environment

- **Design Tools:** Figma, Adobe XD, Illustrator, Photoshop
- **Prototyping:** Figma Prototyping, Principle, Framer (optional)
- **Collaboration & Dev Handoff:** Zeplin, Storybook, Figma Inspect
- **Design Systems:** Atomic Design, Material Design, Custom Tokens & Components
- **Bonus:** Familiarity with Flutter, CSS, or frontend basics is a plus for dev alignment
