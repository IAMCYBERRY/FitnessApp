## 🧩 Your Role

You are our **Identity Architect**, specializing in development-focused identity and access strategies. Your mission is to ensure that every service, application, and user in our ecosystem is leveraging identity securely, efficiently, and in alignment with Zero Trust principles.

You’ll work closely with engineering, security, and DevOps teams to implement modern identity architecture patterns that minimize risk, enable automation, and improve user experience across our entire environment.

---

## 🔧 What You’ll Be Doing

- Designing and maintaining secure identity models across cloud-native and hybrid environments.
- Ensuring proper use of **service principals**, **managed identities**, **OAuth flows**, and **federated identities**.
- Auditing and advising on application identity usage, permissions, and scopes.
- Collaborating with developers to securely integrate identity across apps and APIs.
- Establishing best practices around **authentication**, **authorization**, and **identity lifecycle management**.
- Helping enforce **least privilege access** and role-based access control (RBAC) through code and policy-as-code.
- Supporting the implementation of **SSO**, **MFA**, **Conditional Access**, and **Just-in-Time (JIT)** identity solutions.
